Delete Your Account

Last updated 13 August 2026

You can delete your Bourbon Cup account and its data from inside the app, in two taps, without emailing anybody. This page is the same instruction in writing, plus a way to ask if you can no longer sign in.

In the app

  1. Open the app and sign in as yourself.
  2. Tap More in the bottom bar, then My Account.
  3. Scroll to Account Actions and tap Delete Account.
  4. Two confirmations follow, and they spell out exactly what goes and what stays. The second one is the last chance to stop — after it, the deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

Email aarondjensen@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or name the account you used. Requests are actioned within 30 days and normally the same week.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in. You will not be able to sign in to the app again with it.
  • Your membership — the record that lets your account write anything to the tournament.
  • Your email address, which is the only personal contact detail the app holds, removed from every year's roster.
  • The link between your account and your name on the roster, in every tournament year, not just the current one.
  • Every push notification token registered to you, so notifications stop on every device.

It happens immediately, and it is not recoverable. Signing in again creates a new account from scratch.

What stays, and why

Your name stays on the tournament roster — with the handicap, the scores, and the scorecards signed under it.

That row is not your account. It is a tournament entry the director created, usually before you ever signed in, and it carries holes that the other players in your group attested to. Deleting it would rewrite a result the whole field agreed on at the time, which is not ours to do on one person's request.

What deletion does is unlink that row: your email comes off it, nothing points at your account, and the name is free for somebody to claim again.

Photos you posted also stay unless you remove them first — you can delete your own from the Photos tab at any time, before or instead of deleting the account.

If you want the roster name removed as well, say so in an email to the address above and the director will handle it.

Deleting a guest session

If you looked around the app as a guest, there is nothing to delete. Guest mode creates no account and no record of any kind — it is a single flag stored on your own device. My Account → Exit Guest Mode clears it, and so does clearing the app's site data.
